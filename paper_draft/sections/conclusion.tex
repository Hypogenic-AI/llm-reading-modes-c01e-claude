\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We asked whether multiple production \modes coexist as latent variables in LLM hidden states and whether they are geometrically disentangled. The answer is yes. Across four very different \modes --- 7-way literary authorship, AI-vs-human authorship, formal-vs-casual register, and QWERTY-vs-Dvorak typo pattern --- a single linear probe on \qwen last-token hidden states decodes the latent label at 79.2\%--99.8\% accuracy. The decoded directions are pairwise near-orthogonal ($|\cos| \leq 0.18$), and cross-mode transfer is near chance except for one content-driven overlap (\register $\to$ \aihuman, traceable to ChatGPT's formality bias). Layer-depth profiles further organise the four \modes into three tiers --- shallow content, gradual author, late surface --- that reflect the kind of cue each mode relies on.

\para{Key takeaway.} The \modes abstraction unifies what the AI-text detection, stylometry, and register-classification literatures have treated as separate problems, and shows them to coexist in the same hidden-state geometry without interfering with one another.

\para{Future work.} Three directions are concrete and feasible. (i) \emph{Activation-steering ablation:} subtract the \aihuman probe direction from hidden states during generation and measure perplexity and style shift; this would convert decodability into causal control. (ii) \emph{Cross-model and cross-scale replication:} re-run the pipeline on \llama, Pythia-1B, and \qwen-7B to test whether orthogonality grows with model scale, as linear-features-as-population hypotheses predict. (iii) \emph{More \modes:} time of day of writing, native vs.\ L2 English, dictated (via ASR) vs.\ typed, mobile vs.\ desktop. Each is a one-day extension of our pipeline and would let us ask whether \emph{all} salient production \modes are mutually orthogonal, or only the four we tested.

\para{Reproducibility.} All code is in \texttt{src/} and data download and regeneration instructions are in \texttt{datasets/README.md}. From a clean checkout, the four scripts \texttt{build\_corpora.py}, \texttt{extract\_embeddings.py}, \texttt{probe.py}, and \texttt{merge\_and\_finalize.py} reproduce all numbers and figures in this paper in roughly four hours on a single RTX A6000.
